Information System StSport

Project Overview

This group coursework project focuses on analyzing and designing StSport, a service that connects customers, partner staff, and administrators. The proposed system supports facility reservations, equipment purchases, booking confirmation, and reporting through coordinated workflows.

What the System Addresses

The design aims to organize sports bookings and purchases while reducing scheduling conflicts. Partner staff confirm paid reservations, after which the selected facility and time are blocked to help prevent double booking.

Core Workflows

  • Customer registration, facility booking, and cancellation.
  • Sports equipment ordering.
  • Partner confirmation and reservation status tracking.
  • Reports on facility bookings and equipment sales trends.

Analysis and Design Deliverables

The document includes fishbone analysis, context and data flow diagrams, use cases, class and interaction models, interface designs, and a proposed three-tier architecture. Together, these define the requirements and structure of the proposed system.

System Modeling

The project documents the information exchanged across the application boundary and the steps required to complete a facility booking. These models help define system requirements and coordinate customer, partner staff, and administrator workflows.
